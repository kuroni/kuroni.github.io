Hi! I am currently a fourth-year Ph.D. student at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by [Shuchi Chawla](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~shuchi/). Before joining UT Austin, I completed my BS at Purdue University, working with [Alex Psomas](https://www.alexpsomas.com/) and [Paul Valiant](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/pvaliant/).

I am interested in designing **algorithms under data uncertainty** that still achieves provable guarantees against the best one can do given all the information. Some variants of this theme include *algorithms under noisy data*, *algorithms with few samples*, *algorithms against adversarial data corruption*, and *algorithms with prediction*.

I am also particularly interested in the application of algorithms in economics applications (such as mechanism design and pricing algorithms), and generally interested in approximation algorithms, online algorithms, machine learning theory, and theoretical computer science at large.

My full name in Vietnamese is Đặng Đoàn Đức Trung.
