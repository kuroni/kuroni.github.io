Hi! I am currently a third-year Ph.D. student at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by [Shuchi Chawla](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~shuchi/). Before joining UT Austin, I completed my BS at Purdue University, working with [Alex Psomas](https://www.alexpsomas.com/) and [Paul Valiant](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/pvaliant/).

My primary research interest lies at the intersection of computer science and economics, including mechanism design, pricing algorithms, prophet inequalities and its variants. I am also interested in algorithmic under data uncertainty, approximation algorithms, online algorithms, machine learning theory, and theoretical computer science in general.

My full name in Vietnamese is Đặng Đoàn Đức Trung.
